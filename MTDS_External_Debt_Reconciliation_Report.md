**Reporting cut-off:** 3 November 2026, end of day, Africa/Cairo.  
**Research performed:** 4 November 2026.  
**Scope:** Debt reconciliation, macro-fiscal indicators, and statistical bridge.

**The June 2026 headline totals are verified:** MoF external budget-sector debt of **USD 76,148 million** and CBE gross external debt of **USD 166,748 million**. Their **USD 90,600 million difference is arithmetically verified, but its institutional allocation is not fully reconciled**. [media.mof.gov.eg](https://media.mof.gov.eg/mof-prd/Budget_Sector_Statistical_Debt_Bulletin_June_2026_pptx_b58245a8cd.pdf)

Two corrections are necessary:

- The June 2026 bulletin labels **USD 9,632 million as bilateral loans**, **USD 38,918 million as multilateral loans**, and **USD 144 million as Paris Club debt**. Initial reporting drafts often reverse these labels; “Other/Suppliers” is not the published label.
- The inspected June 2025 bulletin publishes **USD 78,579 million**, producing a June-to-June decline of **USD 2,431 million**. The June 2026 bulletin’s narrative decline of approximately **USD 2,470 million** implies a different starting balance, **USD 78,618 million**, which could not be independently located as a printed June 2025 observation. Both bases are retained below. [media.mof.gov.eg](https://media.mof.gov.eg/mof-prd/Budget_Sector_Statistical_Debt_Bulletin_June_2025_f94b77a1cf.pdf)

**Status convention**

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `VERIFIED` | The observation or statement was located in an inspected official source. This does not guarantee that the source is internally consistent. |
| `PARTIALLY VERIFIED` | Official evidence exists, but access, dating, definition or reconciliation remains incomplete. |
| `NOT VERIFIED` | The observation was not established from the inspected evidence. |
| `CALCULATED` | Analyst arithmetic, explicitly distinguished from a published observation. |

## SECTION B — MTDS targets, pillars and cost-risk benchmarks

### B1. Strategic pillars

The strategy’s funding pillars are: domestic-market development and longer maturities; concessional external financing; reduced international foreign-currency issuance; balanced fixed/floating borrowing; and debt reduction using divestment proceeds. Its fiscal framework additionally emphasizes taxpayer confidence, balanced fiscal policy, integrated debt management, and fiscal space for social programmes and human capital. S01, pp.6 and 33. [media.mof.gov.eg](https://media.mof.gov.eg/mof-prd/Medium_Term_Debt_Strategy_2026_2029_77a7e2fab3.pdf)
